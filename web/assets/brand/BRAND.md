# KikoMix Brand System

Canonical reference: `brand-board.jpg` (supplied by Nathan, 2026-09-22).
All masters live in `web/assets/brand/`.

## Colors

- **Black** `#000000` — primary background. The brand lives on black.
- **White** `#FFFFFF` — marks, logotype, primary text.
- **Signature orange** `#FF8000` — the waveform, halo ring, gi accents. The single
  brand accent. Replaces the old gold (`#e3a008`) everywhere it represented KikoMix.
- **Alternate blue** `#009FFF` — app-icon alternate only, not a brand accent.

## Marks (in priority order)

1. **Primary mark** (`logo-primary.jpg`): white hands forming a triangle/diamond
   around vertical orange waveform bars. Default logo everywhere.
2. **Logotype** (`logotype.jpg`): `KIKOMIX` in bold geometric sans; the `O` is a
   circle containing the orange waveform. For headers, splash, and wordmark use.
3. **Character, full body** (`character-full.jpg`): martial-arts figure in white gi
   with orange accents and halo ring, hands in the triangle-waveform gesture.
   For hero moments, welcome/empty states, feature headers.
4. **Character, bust** (`character-bust.jpg`): portrait version. For Now Playing
   art, feature/section marks, LionDavid-adjacent moments.
5. **App icons**: dark tile primary (`web/icons/dark/`), orange and blue tiles as
   user-selectable alternates (`web/icons/orange/`, `web/icons/blue/`).

## Usage rules

- Marks sit on black or very dark surfaces. Never on light backgrounds.
- The waveform bars are always orange on dark, white on the orange/blue tiles.
- Character art is original (Tien Shinhan-inspired). Technique names drawn from
  anime still need IP review before public launch — art itself is ours.
- In-app brand picker lets users switch icon variant + accent; the installed
  PWA home-screen icon is fixed by the OS and cannot change dynamically.
- Keep generous clear space; never stretch, recolor, or add gradients to the marks.
